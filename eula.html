  • As is. Weather data is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Forecasts may be inaccurate, incomplete or late, and conditions can change faster than a forecast updates.
  • General information only. Forecasts and Brick's suggestions are for general information about planning your training. Don't rely on them for decisions about your safety, or anyone else's. Check the conditions yourself before and during any outdoor session, and follow local warnings and official advice.
  • No open-water assessment. Brick never assesses open-water conditions and never tells you whether it's safe to swim. A water temperature shown for a swim is only the posted temperature you typed, as you typed it; Brick doesn't check it. Follow the rules, lifeguards and posted conditions at your swim venue.

14. Location

Forecasts are for the places you save for outdoor sessions: a place you type, found with Apple Maps, or your current location when you tap Current Location, which grants Brick location access for that one use. Brick sends a saved place's coordinates to Apple Weather to fetch its forecast, and your place searches to Apple Maps to find matches, with nothing that identifies you. Brick never tracks your location and never reads it in the background. The Brick Privacy Policy (privacy-policy.md, hosted at https://paradigmshift6.github.io/brick-triathlon/privacy.html) explains what is stored and sent, and Apple's privacy policy governs Apple's handling of those requests.
