# Brick: Triathlon Training

The support page and legal documents for Brick, the triathlon training app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch published by Fireside WiFi LLC, served by GitHub Pages at https://paradigmshift6.github.io/brick-triathlon/.

- `index.html`: the support page (the App Store Support URL)
- `privacy.html`: the Brick Privacy Policy
- `terms.html`: the Brick Terms of Use
- `eula.html`: the Brick End User License Agreement

The pages are generated from `docs/privacy-policy.md`, `docs/terms-of-use.md` and `docs/eula.md` in the app's repository, without their top comments. Edit those files and regenerate the pages rather than editing the HTML here.
